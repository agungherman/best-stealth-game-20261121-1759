The Silent Stacks

Night has fallen on the Grand Archive. Three rare manuscripts sleep behind the shelves — and the night wardens walk their rounds with lanterns.

Stay out of the lantern cones. Slip behind shelves and crates. Toss a coin to draw a warden away. Take every manuscript, then reach the green door.

Move: WASD / Arrow keys  ·  Sneak: hold Shift  ·  Toss coin: E or mouse click

Wardens patrol fixed rounds — learn them. A yellow cone means a warden can see you; a red cone means you have been spotted. A "?" means a warden is coming to look. A "!" means run for cover. Footsteps grow louder as a warden draws near.
